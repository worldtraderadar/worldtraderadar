## 1. Failure analysis (V5.10 live Qwen, DECISION=0)

Keys (14): `price_pressure`, `raise_price`, `supplier_choice`, `competitor_pressure`, `new_country`, `keep_customer`, `moq`, `incoterms`, `uncertain_data`, `quote_conversion`, `repeat_orders`, `distributor_pick`, `lost_quote`, `say_no`.

Pattern: partial context → Qwen opened with clarification questions instead of a provisional hold.

| Cluster | Keys | What Qwen did |
|---|---|---|
| Price / terms without metrics | `raise_price`, `price_pressure`, `competitor_pressure`, `moq`, `incoterms`, `lost_quote` | Asked for marj/MOQ/navlun instead of hold (no hike, no cut, FOB, no match) |
| Binary pick with empty cards | `supplier_choice`, `distributor_pick`, `uncertain_data`, `new_country`, `keep_customer` | Re-asked product/channel instead of keeping the known side |
| Diagnostic / threshold | `quote_conversion`, `repeat_orders`, `say_no` | Diagnosed, then stopped at “need more variables” |

Root trigger: CMO said “if critical data is missing, ask 1–3 questions.” The commercial brief listed those questions **before** stance, and decision cases without `session.product` always injected “Hangi ürün ve müşteri segmenti…?”

## 2. Contract / prompt nudges

- `CMO_CONTRACT`: missing metrics still require an **explicit provisional stance first**; questions come after (max 1–3). Do not open or finish with a question list. V5.7.1 line kept.
- `DECISION_CONTRACT` / `DIAGNOSTIC_CONTRACT`: stance before missing-variable list; do not open with a question.
- `commercial.py` brief: `ÖNCE DURUŞ` → trade-off / next step → missing data / questions.
- Generic product question is not injected when a stance already exists.
- Partial-choice holds (price hike, MOQ, supplier/distributor, FOB, credit/accept, conversion, leave-market) use existing scorer verbs. Evaluator was not widened.

Llama3 default, canary percent, SHA-256 routing, validator REJECT rules: unchanged.
